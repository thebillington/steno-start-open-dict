# Sources

The registry is `sources/sources.json`.

Allowed inputs:

- CMUdict, BSD-2-Clause, pronunciation only.
- Art of Chording, CC BY-SA 4.0, https://www.artofchording.com/.
- Wikipedia: Stenotype, CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Plover Wiki pages listed in the registry, CC BY as stated in the wiki footer.
- Steno Start authored word list and rules.

Excluded:

- Plover main.json and derivatives.
- Typey Type and other GPL dictionaries.
- Lapwing dictionary until its licence is clarified.
- StenEd, Magnum, Phoenix or other proprietary dictionaries.
- User-generated dictionaries of unclear provenance.

The Plover Wiki is a public theory reference, not the GPL dictionary file. The
pages used are recorded with URL, licence and SHA-256. Page-level provenance must
be reviewed before commercial release.
