This book's goal is to enable anyone to learn stenography (or steno, for short.) # Stenography Stenography is the fastest way to write on a computer. Court reporters have used machine stenography since the late 1800s to create written records of human speech in real-time. A steno machine can completely replace keyboard usage. This book is part of the Open Steno Project (opens new window) , whose mission is to bring stenography to everyone .
