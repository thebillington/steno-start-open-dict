This lesson covers the prefix compound that begins words like "enter" and "interact". We use just a single cluster for both "ent-" and "int-" briefs. The combination is arbitrary, SPW : # S T K P W H R A O * E U F R P B L G T S D Z SPWER : enter SPWAOEUR : entire SPWAEUL : entail SPWAOEUS : entice SPWEL : intel A little more complex: SPWABGT : intact SPWEPBD : intend SPWEPBGS : intention SPWERPB : intern SPWERPBL : internal SPWERPBT : internet We can add in sounds on the left hand, too: SPWRUPT : interupt SPWRABGS : interaction SPWREU : entry SPWRAPBS : entrance SPWHREBGT : intellect # Briefs Chord Translation Notes SPWELGT intelligent SPWELGS intelligence SPWOURPBLG entourage SPWROUS introduce SPWERPT interpret PROU produce PROUS produces PROUR producer PROUG producing ← -lge, -nge, -lk, -nk Multistroke words →
