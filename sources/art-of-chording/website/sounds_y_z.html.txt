This lesson covers how you write initial Y and Z in steno, like in the words "zoo" and "young". # Left side Y Left side Y is given by the outline KWR . # S T K P W H R A O * E U F R P B L G T S D Z KWREP : yep KWRUPL : yum KWROUR : your Do not use KWRUR , you will need it later. KWROUPBG : young KWRORBG : York KWROE : yo # Left side Z Left side Z is given by the outline S* .
