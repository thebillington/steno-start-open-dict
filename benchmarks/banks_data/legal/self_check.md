## Method notes

- Each word is assigned exactly one single-stroke outline in `data/dictionary.json`.
- `data/multi_stroke.json` is an optional expansion layer for words with more than one stressed vowel; it is not needed to cover the word list.
- Briefs are reserved before rules. The briefs layer is documented in `generator/lib/src/briefs.dart` and `generator/RULES.md`.
- Rule-derived entries cite `cmudict`, `wikipedia-stenotype`, `steno-start-word-list` and `steno-start-rules`; entries whose exact example appears in the archived Art of Chording text also cite `art-of-chording-website`.
- The generator does not read or include Plover `main.json`, GPL dictionaries or proprietary dictionary data. The Plover Wiki pages are public theory references, not the GPL dictionary.
